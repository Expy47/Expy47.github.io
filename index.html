— RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

Academic Thesis

🚇 Access-Egress Mode Choice Modeling for Dhaka MRT Line 6

Supervisor: Dr. Annesha Enam

Investigated access-egress mode choice behavior for commuters of Dhaka's first mass rapid transit (MRT Line 6) using a purpose-built revealed preference survey conducted at all 16 metro stations. Combined the survey data with travel time and distance extracted via the Google Maps Distance Matrix API, and applied Multinomial Logit (MNL) and Mixed Multinomial Logit (MMNL) models — estimated using the Apollo package in R — to model mode choice behavior.

Tools: R (Apollo package), Python, Google Maps Distance Matrix API

This research was funded by the Research and Innovation Centre for Science and Engineering (RISE) grant.
